ITA

Dr. Eng. Raffaele Mineo è Research Fellow presso il Department of Industrial, Electrical, Electronic and Computer Engineering (DIEEI) dell'Università di Catania e membro del PeRCeiVe.AI Lab. Ha conseguito il National PhD in Artificial Intelligence - Health and Life Sciences. La sua ricerca riguarda medical AI, imaging cardiovascolare e fisiologia virtuale, sensing multimodale e radar, riconoscimento della Lingua dei Segni Italiana, continual e federated learning, prompt-based adaptation e metodi di efficient AI quali knowledge distillation, quantizzazione, sparsificazione e pruning. È Associate Editor di Frontiers in Communication. Nel 2026 è Area Chair di MICCAI, fa parte dell'Organizing Committee di IEEE ISBI come Associate to Technical Program Chairs e ha svolto il ruolo di Session Chair a EEAI. Nel 2027 è Area Chair di IJCNN, IEEE FG e IEEE ICASSP e fa parte dell'Organizing Committee di IEEE ISBI come Associate to Technical Program Chairs. È inoltre inventore di una famiglia brevettuale per la stima AI-based della fisiologia coronarica da angiografia.

ENG

Dr. Eng. Raffaele Mineo is a Research Fellow at the Department of Industrial, Electrical, Electronic and Computer Engineering (DIEEI), University of Catania, and a member of the PeRCeiVe.AI Lab. He completed the National PhD in Artificial Intelligence - Health and Life Sciences. His research spans medical AI, cardiovascular imaging and virtual physiology, multimodal and radar sensing, Italian Sign Language recognition, continual and federated learning, prompt-based adaptation, and efficient AI methods including knowledge distillation, quantization, sparsification and pruning. He is an Associate Editor of Frontiers in Communication. In 2026, he served as Area Chair for MICCAI, as Associate to Technical Program Chairs on the IEEE ISBI Organizing Committee, and as Session Chair at EEAI. In 2027, he serves as Area Chair for IJCNN, IEEE FG and IEEE ICASSP and as Associate to Technical Program Chairs on the IEEE ISBI Organizing Committee. He is also an inventor on a patent family for AI-based coronary physiology estimation from angiography.

ITA

Dr. Eng. Raffaele Mineo è Research Fellow presso il Department of Industrial, Electrical, Electronic and Computer Engineering (DIEEI) dell'Università di Catania e membro del PeRCeiVe.AI Lab. Ha conseguito il National PhD in Artificial Intelligence - Health and Life Sciences. La sua ricerca riguarda medical AI, imaging cardiovascolare e fisiologia virtuale, sensing multimodale e radar, riconoscimento della Lingua dei Segni Italiana, continual e federated learning, prompt-based adaptation e metodi di efficient AI quali knowledge distillation, quantizzazione, sparsificazione e pruning. È Associate Editor di Frontiers in Communication, Area Chair di MICCAI 2026 e IJCNN 2027 e inventore di una famiglia brevettuale per la stima AI-based della fisiologia coronarica da angiografia.

ENG

Dr. Eng. Raffaele Mineo is a Research Fellow at the Department of Industrial, Electrical, Electronic and Computer Engineering (DIEEI), University of Catania, and a member of the PeRCeiVe.AI Lab. He completed the National PhD in Artificial Intelligence - Health and Life Sciences. His research spans medical AI, cardiovascular imaging and virtual physiology, multimodal and radar sensing, Italian Sign Language recognition, continual and federated learning, prompt-based adaptation, and efficient AI methods including knowledge distillation, quantization, sparsification and pruning. He is an Associate Editor of Frontiers in Communication, an Area Chair for MICCAI 2026 and IJCNN 2027, and an inventor on a patent family for AI-based coronary physiology estimation from angiography.

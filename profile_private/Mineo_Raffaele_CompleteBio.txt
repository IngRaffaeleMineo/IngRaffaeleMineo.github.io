ITA - BIO COMPLETA
======================================================================

Dr. Eng. Raffaele Mineo è Research Fellow in Artificial Intelligence presso il Department of Industrial, Electrical, Electronic and Computer Engineering (DIEEI) dell'Università di Catania. Dal 2019 svolge attività di ricerca nel PeRCeiVe.AI Lab / comunità CVPL, con interessi che comprendono computer vision, medical AI, imaging cardiovascolare e fisiologia computazionale, sensing multimodale e radar, riconoscimento della Lingua dei Segni Italiana, neurocognitive AI, continual e federated learning, prompt-based adaptation, knowledge distillation, quantizzazione, sparsificazione, pruning, embedded AI e sistemi intelligenti per applicazioni scientifiche e industriali.

Ha completato nel 2026 il National PhD in Artificial Intelligence - Health and Life Sciences presso l'University Campus Bio-Medico of Rome, con affiliazione all'Università di Catania. In precedenza ha conseguito la Laurea Magistrale in Computer Engineering all'Università di Catania nel 2021 con 110/110 e lode, la Laurea Triennale in Computer Engineering nel 2019 con 99/110 e il diploma di Liceo Scientifico nel 2016 con 97/100.

La sua traiettoria di ricerca parte dall'analisi di segnali cerebrali e neuroimaging EEG/fMRI (2019-2020), prosegue con imaging toracico e analisi polmonare (2020), anomaly detection e sismologia vulcanica con INGV/EUROVOLC (2020-2021), imaging cardiovascolare e fisiologia computazionale con stima non invasiva di FFR/iFR da angiografia coronarica (2022-2023), riconoscimento multimodale della Lingua dei Segni Italiana e sensing privacy-aware basato anche su radar (2023-2026), digital cognitive assessment (2024), prompt-based adaptation, continual/incremental learning e federated learning (2025), fino ai temi di model compression ed efficient AI nel 2026, comprendenti knowledge distillation, post-training e mixed-precision quantization, sparsification e pruning.

La linea cardiovascolare ha portato alla famiglia brevettuale "Angiographic image processing method and system", comprendente il brevetto italiano n. 102024000014434, concesso da UIBM il 20 luglio 2026, e l'estensione internazionale PCT/IB2025/056252 pubblicata come WO 2025/262634 A1. La famiglia protegge l'elaborazione angiografica AI-based alla base della linea di ricerca STARFLOW.

L'attività di servizio scientifico comprende il ruolo di Associate Editor di Frontiers in Communication dal 2026 e di Lead Guest Editor per Cognitive Computation nel 2026, oltre a ruoli di Area Chair, program leadership, organizzazione di workshop, challenge e special session. Ha svolto attività di reviewing per journal, conference e workshop internazionali, includendo IEEE Transactions on Medical Imaging, Pattern Recognition, Nature Communications, Frontiers in Digital Health, Frontiers in Medicine, IEEE Transactions on Biomedical Engineering e numerose altre sedi riportate integralmente nella sezione canonica seguente.

Svolge inoltre attività didattica come Teaching Tutor presso l'Università di Catania, mentoring e research advising, e partecipa a società scientifiche e technical communities IEEE, ACM e CVPL. La sua attività comprende anche collaborazioni scientifiche e industriali con INGV, AOU Città della Salute e della Scienza di Torino, Max Planck Institute for Intelligent Systems, STMicroelectronics R&D Power and Discrete e DeepSensing S.r.l.

Le competenze tecniche coprono AI e data science, programmazione, sistemi embedded e sensing, sviluppo software, web e networking, strumenti di engineering e media/design. Il profilo comprende inoltre esperienza pratica in riparazione elettronica e informatica, impianti, cablaggio, saldatura, falegnameria, relazioni tecnico-estimative e perizie informatiche.

ENG - COMPLETE BIO
======================================================================

Dr. Eng. Raffaele Mineo is a Research Fellow in Artificial Intelligence at the Department of Industrial, Electrical, Electronic and Computer Engineering (DIEEI), University of Catania. Since 2019, he has carried out research within the PeRCeiVe.AI Lab / CVPL research community, with interests spanning computer vision, medical AI, cardiovascular imaging and computational physiology, multimodal and radar sensing, Italian Sign Language recognition, neurocognitive AI, continual and federated learning, prompt-based adaptation, knowledge distillation, quantization, sparsification, pruning, embedded AI, and intelligent systems for scientific and industrial applications.

He completed the National PhD in Artificial Intelligence - Health and Life Sciences in 2026 at the University Campus Bio-Medico of Rome, with University of Catania affiliation. He previously obtained an M.Sc. in Computer Engineering from the University of Catania in 2021 with 110/110 cum laude, a B.Sc. in Computer Engineering in 2019 with 99/110, and a Scientific High School Diploma in 2016 with 97/100.

His research trajectory began with brain-signal analysis and EEG/fMRI neuroimaging (2019-2020), followed by thoracic imaging and lung analysis (2020), anomaly detection and volcanic seismology with INGV/EUROVOLC (2020-2021), cardiovascular imaging and computational physiology with non-invasive FFR/iFR estimation from coronary angiography (2022-2023), multimodal Italian Sign Language recognition and privacy-aware sensing including radar (2023-2026), digital cognitive assessment (2024), prompt-based adaptation, continual/incremental learning and federated learning (2025), and model compression and efficient AI in 2026, including knowledge distillation, post-training and mixed-precision quantization, sparsification and pruning.

The cardiovascular research line led to the patent family "Angiographic image processing method and system", including Italian patent no. 102024000014434, granted by UIBM on 20 July 2026, and international PCT application PCT/IB2025/056252, published as WO 2025/262634 A1. The family protects AI-based angiographic processing underpinning the STARFLOW research line.

His scientific service includes serving as Associate Editor of Frontiers in Communication since 2026 and Lead Guest Editor of Cognitive Computation in 2026, together with area-chair, program-leadership, workshop, challenge and special-session organization roles. He has reviewed for international journals, conferences and workshops, including IEEE Transactions on Medical Imaging, Pattern Recognition, Nature Communications, Frontiers in Digital Health, Frontiers in Medicine, IEEE Transactions on Biomedical Engineering, and the full set of venues listed in the canonical profile below.

He also contributes to teaching as a Teaching Tutor at the University of Catania, mentoring and research advising, and participates in IEEE, ACM and CVPL scientific societies and technical communities. His collaborations include INGV, AOU Città della Salute e della Scienza in Turin, the Max Planck Institute for Intelligent Systems, STMicroelectronics R&D Power and Discrete, and DeepSensing S.r.l.

His technical background spans AI and data science, programming, embedded systems and sensing, software engineering, web and networking, engineering tools, and media/design. The profile also includes hands-on experience in electronics and computer/device repair, installations, structured cabling, welding, carpentry, technical-estimative reports and IT appraisals.

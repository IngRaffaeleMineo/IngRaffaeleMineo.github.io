Profile

About

Computer vision and artificial intelligence for healthcare, multimodal sensing and efficient learning.

I work at the intersection of computer vision, medical artificial intelligence and multimodal sensing. A recurring theme in my research is the extraction of meaningful latent information from data that are incomplete, privacy-sensitive or constrained by real-world acquisition and hardware conditions.

My work has addressed non-invasive coronary physiology from angiographic videos, radar-based sign language recognition for healthcare communication, vision-language adaptation, continual and federated learning, and efficient inference on embedded platforms. I also contribute to scientific service through area-chair, editorial, reviewing and conference-organization roles.

Research

Research areas

The timeline summarizes the main application domains and methods I have worked on since 2019.

Medical AI and computational physiology

Cardiovascular imaging, coronary physiology, cognitive assessment and biomedical modeling from clinical images, gaze and behavioral signals.

Multimodal sensing and inclusive communication

RGB, depth, skeleton and radar sensing for Italian Sign Language recognition, accessible healthcare communication and privacy-aware perception.

Adaptive, efficient and deployable AI

Federated and continual learning, prompt-based adaptation, post-training quantization, knowledge distillation, sparsification, pruning and edge deployment.

Scientific and industrial sensing

EEG and physiological signals, volcanic-seismic analysis, industrial inspection, predictive reliability and machine-learning systems beyond clinical imaging.

Timeline

Research topics by year

2026

Model compression & efficient AI

Knowledge distillation, post-training and mixed-precision quantization, sparsification and pruning for efficient vision models and deployment under compute constraints.

2025

Prompt-based adaptation & federated/continual learning

Vision-language prompt tuning, personalized continual/incremental learning and federated learning for data-efficient medical image analysis.

2024

Digital cognitive assessment

Computer vision for cognitive screening, including facial micro-movement analysis and eye-gaze modeling for automated neurocognitive assessment.

2023–2026

Italian Sign Language (LIS) & privacy-aware multimodal sensing

Multimodal LIS recognition for healthcare communication using RGB, depth, skeleton and radar sensing; MultiMedaLIS, radar-based recognition and SignEval benchmarks/challenges.

2022–2023

Cardiovascular imaging & computational physiology

Coronary angiography, stenosis assessment and non-invasive FFR/iFR estimation, establishing the methodological foundations of the STARFLOW research line.

2020–2021

Volcanic seismology & anomaly detection

Deep learning for seismic anomaly detection and volcanic-unrest analysis with INGV/EUROVOLC, including the VolUnD toolkit and machine-learning database activities.

2020

Thoracic imaging & lung analysis

Deep-learning and computer-vision methods for lung and thoracic imaging, extending early medical-imaging work beyond neuroimaging.

2019–2020

Brain signal analysis & neuroimaging (EEG/fMRI)

Early research on brain data and representation learning across EEG and fMRI, with emphasis on spatio-temporal patterns and neuroimaging analysis.

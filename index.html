About

Computer vision and artificial intelligence for healthcare, multimodal sensing and efficient learning.

I work at the intersection of computer vision, medical artificial intelligence and multimodal sensing. A recurring theme in my research is the extraction of meaningful latent information from data that are incomplete, privacy-sensitive or constrained by real-world acquisition and hardware conditions.

My work has addressed non-invasive coronary physiology from angiographic videos, radar-based sign language recognition for healthcare communication, vision-language adaptation, continual and federated learning, and efficient inference on embedded platforms. I also contribute to scientific service through area-chair, editorial, reviewing and conference-organization roles.
